### Bezpieczna Ojczyzna - CASSINI Hackathon

Built in 24 hours during CASSINI Hackathon.

A working command-center prototype for national security and critical infrastructure monitoring. The system connected:

1. citizen field reports from a mobile app
2. satellite-based monitoring around critical infrastructure
3. operator dashboard for triage and response management

Technical stack and concepts:

`Python/FastAPI` · `PocketBase` · `Leaflet` · `YOLO` · `NLP risk scoring` · `LLaVA-style multimodal analysis` · `Sentinel imagery` · `OpenCV` · `geospatial processing`

During the live demo, the entire room could submit reports from their phones. The dashboard instantly displayed incidents on the map, enriched with AI-generated tags, reliability signals, risk levels and geospatial context.

It was not slideware. It was a functioning end-to-end geospatial intelligence workflow.
